Our Story & Purpose

Rooted Across Rural Illinois

Founded in November 2023, The Rural Equality Project was founded on one simple idea; To Promote Diversity and Equity in Rural Illinois, Through Outreach, Community Building and Advocacy.

Accessible Health & Crisis Resources

Navigating support systems in rural areas can be challenging. We maintain a directory of state, nationwide, and local hotlines, healthcare providers, and mutual aid organizations.

Pekin Pride Festival & Outreach

Launching the inaugural Pekin Pride Festival in August 2024 marked a historic milestone for Tazewell County. We create family-friendly events that bring neighbors, local vendors, and advocates together.

Coalition & Civic Engagement

We partner with organizations across Central Illinois to strengthen regional solidarity, educate communities, and amplify rural LGBTQ+ voices.

Pekin Pride Festival

First launched in August 2024, the Pekin Pride Festival aims to bring families, neighbors, local artisans, and regional advocates together in the heart of Pekin to celebrate one another.

When the Rural Equality Project organized the inaugural Pekin Pride Festival in August 2024, it created a welcoming, joyous, and affirming space right in Tazewell County. Designed from the ground up to be family-friendly and accessible to all ages, the festival proves that pride and community solidarity thrive in small towns too.

Each festival features a vibrant mix of local craft vendors, food trucks, live music and performances, youth and family activities, and an outreach fair connecting residents directly with health and community organizations.
